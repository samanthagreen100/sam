# Common Ground

A lightweight, dependency-free starter site for organizing and drafting common legal documents.

## Use

Open `index.html` in a browser. Edit the prompts in the page to suit your project, then replace them with reviewed, accurate policy language before publishing or signing anything.

The included privacy, terms of service, and cookie notice sections are drafting prompts only. They are not legal advice or complete legal documents. Legal requirements depend on your project and the locations where you operate; have a qualified legal professional review your drafts.

## Customize

- Update the site name and introductory copy in `index.html`.
- Edit or remove document sections to match your needs.
- Adjust colors, typography, and responsive layouts in `styles.css`.

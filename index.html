The library

Start with the essentials

Use these outlines to collect the details a reviewer will need. Delete prompts that do not apply and add anything specific to your project.

01 / PRIVACY

Privacy notice

Explain what personal information you handle and what people can expect.

Drafting prompts

  • Who is responsible for this service? [Organization name and contact]
  • What information do you collect, and when? [List data and collection points]
  • Why do you use it, and what is your legal basis? [Purpose and applicable basis]
  • Who receives it, and how long is it kept? [Providers, sharing, and retention]
  • How can people exercise their rights? [Process and contact details]
02 / TERMS

Terms of service

Set out the ground rules for accessing and using your product or service.

Drafting prompts

  • Who can use the service? [Eligibility and account requirements]
  • What does the service provide? [Scope, limits, and changes]
  • What conduct is not allowed? [Acceptable use expectations]
  • How do payment, cancellation, and refunds work? [If applicable]
  • How are disputes and account closures handled? [Review with counsel]
03 / COOKIES

Cookie notice

Describe cookies and similar technologies used on your site or application.

Drafting prompts

  • Which technologies are in use? [Name, purpose, and provider]
  • Which are essential, and which are optional? [Categories and justification]
  • How can visitors choose or change settings? [Consent and preference controls]
  • How long do they remain active? [Duration for each technology]
